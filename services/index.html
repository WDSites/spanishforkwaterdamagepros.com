Water Damage Restoration Services in Spanish Fork, UT

Six services, one 24/7 emergency line. Pick where your situation starts and we will explain the rest.

☎ Call (385) 988-9410

Water damage restoration in Spanish Fork is a chain of services, and most losses use several of them in order. The emergency line, open 24 hours a day, routes you to the right starting point whether water is still spreading or the damage is already dry.

If water is still coming in, start with emergency water removal. If the source is stopped and walls and floors are wet, water mitigation explains drying. Mold, flood and rebuild work each have their own page below.

Water Damage Repair

Rebuild after drying: drywall, insulation, flooring, trim and paint, with a written scope.

Water Mitigation

Damage control in the first 24 to 72 hours: stop the source, extract, map moisture and dry.

Water Damage Cleanup

Debris, contaminated materials, contents and odors handled once the water is stopped.

Emergency Water Removal

Pumps and extraction units for standing water in basements, kitchens and living spaces.

Flood Damage Restoration

Runoff, river overflow, groundwater and sewer backups, including basement and crawl space losses.

Mold Remediation

Inspection, containment, removal and verification following ANSI/IICRC S520.

How the services fit together

A typical loss moves through the pages in a fixed order. Water is stopped and extracted first, then the structure is dried and monitored. Debris and contaminated materials come out during cleanup, and rebuilding starts only after readings show the structure is dry.

Some losses skip steps. A small clean-water leak caught within hours may need only drying. A sewer backup adds contamination and heavier cleanup, and a slow leak found behind a wall may start at inspection and mold work. The dispatcher asks a few questions so the first visit matches the problem.

Whatever the starting point, we explain the scope and price before work starts. You will know what is included, what is separate and what is a different trade, such as plumbing or foundation drainage.

Privacy Policy

How we handle information from calls and from this website.

Overview

This policy explains how Spanish Fork Water Damage Pros handles information when you visit this website or call our line. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

Information from phone calls

When you call, we may ask for your name, address, phone number and a description of the damage so we can arrange help. We use that information to respond to your request, to explain the work and to follow up on your call.

Information from this website

This site has no forms and no user accounts. Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and the pages visited, to keep the site running and secure. Embedded maps and fonts may set their own cookies under the policies of the companies that provide them.

How we share information

We share your details only with the restoration professionals and service providers who need them to respond to your call, and where the law requires it. We do not use call information for unrelated purposes.

Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about a call or ask us to correct it by emailing contact@spanishforkwaterdamagepros.com. We will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

We may update this policy from time to time, and the current version is always on this page.

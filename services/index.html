Water Damage Restoration Services in Spanish Fork, UT

Six core services and sixteen pages for specific causes, rooms and materials. One 24/7 emergency line covers all of them.

☎ Call (385) 988-9410

Water damage restoration in Spanish Fork is a chain of services, and most losses use several of them in order. The emergency line, open 24 hours a day, routes you to the right starting point whether water is still spreading or the damage is already dry.

If water is still coming in, start with emergency water removal. If the source is stopped and walls and floors are wet, water mitigation explains drying. The groups below match a specific cause, room or material to its own page.

Core services

Water Damage Repair

Rebuild after drying: drywall, insulation, flooring, trim and paint, with a written scope.

Water Mitigation

Damage control in the first 24 to 72 hours: stop the source, extract, map moisture and dry.

Water Damage Cleanup

Debris, contaminated materials, contents and odors handled once the water is stopped.

Emergency Water Removal

Pumps and extraction units for standing water in basements, kitchens and living spaces.

Flood Damage Restoration

Runoff, river overflow, groundwater and sewer backups, including basement and crawl space losses.

Mold Remediation

Inspection, containment, removal and verification following ANSI/IICRC S520.

By cause

Start here when you know what failed. Each page covers the first hour, the crew process and the insurance angle for that source.

Burst Pipe Water Damage

A supply line that fails under pressure: shut-off, extraction, wall and ceiling drying, and what the plumber handles.

Frozen Pipe Water Damage

Pipes that split in the cold and leak on the thaw, with Spanish Fork frost depth and cold snap steps.

Roof Leak Water Damage

Water that came through shingles, flashing or an ice dam: wet insulation, stained ceilings and attic drying.

Water Heater Leak Cleanup

A tank or fitting that failed and spread water across a utility room, closet or finished floor.

Appliance Leak Water Damage

Washer, dishwasher and refrigerator line leaks that soak floors and cabinet bases behind the machine.

AC Leak Water Damage Repair

Clogged condensate lines and full pans that drip on ceilings and attic floors, with an honest note on how common they are.

Slab Leak Water Damage

A line under or inside a concrete floor, how uncommon that is in basement country and what we dry above it.

Sewage Backup Cleanup

Category 3 backups through floor drains and toilets, with protective gear, removal and sanitizing.

By room or place

Pick the space that is wet when the cause is still unclear.

Kitchen Water Damage Restoration

Sink, dishwasher and refrigerator leaks that reach cabinets, counters and flooring together.

Basement Water Damage Cleanup

Window wells, sump failures and groundwater in finished and unfinished basements.

Crawl Space Water Damage

Standing water, wet insulation and damp joists in the space under the floor.

Commercial Water Damage Restoration

Water losses in commercial buildings: extraction, drying, cleanup and a clear written record.

By material

Floors react differently to water, and the repair decision depends on the surface.

Hardwood Floor Water Damage Repair

Cupping, crowning and buckled boards, with drying mats and an honest repair or replace answer.

Carpet Water Damage Restoration

Wet carpet and pad: what can be dried in place, what has to come out and why water category matters.

Storm and structure

Weather events and the drying work that applies to every wet structure.

Storm Water Damage

Rain, runoff and wind driven water that enters through roofs, windows, wells and thresholds.

Structural Drying and Dehumidification

How drying is planned and measured: air movers, dehumidifiers, drying goals and daily readings.

How the services fit together

A typical loss moves through the pages in a fixed order. Water is stopped and extracted first, then the structure is dried and monitored. Debris and contaminated materials come out during cleanup, and rebuilding starts only after readings show the structure is dry.

Some losses skip steps. A small clean-water leak caught within hours may need only drying. A sewer backup adds contamination and heavier cleanup, and a slow leak found behind a wall may start at inspection and mold work. The dispatcher asks a few questions so the first visit matches the problem.

Whatever the starting point, we explain the scope and price before work starts. You will know what is included, what is separate and what is a different trade, such as plumbing or foundation drainage.

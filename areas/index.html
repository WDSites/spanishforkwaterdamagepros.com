Water Damage Service Areas Around Spanish Fork, UT

One emergency line for Spanish Fork and five nearby Utah County towns.

☎ Call (385) 988-9410

Spanish Fork is our home base at 1061 S Main St, and the same 24/7 emergency line covers the towns around it. Each town below has its own page with local water exposure, housing and ZIP codes.

We serve Spanish Fork itself with every service on our services page. If you are on the edge of town or in a neighboring community, call and tell us where the water is. Same-day response is available when a crew is available.

Springville

Northeast of Spanish Fork, along Hobble Creek. Creek-side basements and canals shape most calls.

Salem

South of Spanish Fork, with canal-fed irrigation and ponds. Two separate water systems to keep straight.

Payson

Southwest of Spanish Fork, down the valley. Older homes and newer subdivisions in one town.

Mapleton

Northeast of Spanish Fork, beyond Springville. Hard well water and fast growth.

Provo

North of Spanish Fork, a larger city along I-15 with a wide range of housing.

Smaller neighbors

Lake Shore, Elk Ridge, Woodland Hills and Benjamin sit within reach of the same line. We do not have separate pages for them, so call and we will confirm service for your address. Lake Shore is an unincorporated community, while Elk Ridge and Woodland Hills are separate small cities.

What to have ready when you call

Knowing your street address, where the water started, whether it has stopped and roughly how much floor is wet helps the dispatcher plan. If you can, take photos before moving anything. If power is involved, tell us before you do anything else.

If the water is clearly still spreading, do not wait on this page. Call the line and ask what to do while the crew is arranged.
